# Taste — Aesthetic Discernment & Visual Refinement

Design taste is the art of knowing what to omit. Craft interfaces that look human-designed, luxurious, and intentional.

## Design Tenets
1. **Restraint Over Clutter**:
   - Avoid generic AI clichés: oversized meaningless gradient blobs, excessive floating badges, unmotivated icons.
   - Use accents (`--m-aqua`, gold/emerald highlights) deliberately for actions and statuses, never flooding the canvas.
2. **Typographic Rhythm**:
   - Limit to 2 font families (Clean Sans-Serif + Monospace for timings).
   - Generous whitespace and proportional leading so dense competition data breathes naturally.
3. **Intentional Surfaces**:
   - Every border, shadow, and radius must feel unified.
   - Consistent corner radii (`rounded-2xl` for containers, `rounded-xl` for interactive cards, `rounded-lg` for buttons).
4. **Document Dignity**:
   - Official printed certificates, invoices, meet books, and official letters must look authoritative, balanced, and ready for institutional sanction.
